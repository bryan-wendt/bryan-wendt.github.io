# Non-Destructive Bypass Techniques

Bypass methods target the structural, mechanical, or sensory vulnerabilities surrounding a locking system without manipulating the lock cylinder itself.

---

## 1. Under-Door Tool (UDT) Attack

- **Principle:** Reaching beneath the door undercut to manipulate the interior egress handle or crash bar.
- **Target Hardware:** [Commercial Lever Handles](/docs/obstacles/egress-hardware) on [Outswing Doors](/docs/obstacles/doors-and-frames).
- **Execution:**
  1. Slide the semi-rigid wire base under the door threshold.
  2. Align the looped cable head over the interior lever handle using camera or mirror guidance.
  3. Pull the internal pull-string to tighten and depress the lever, withdrawing the latch bolt.

---

## 2. Latch Slipping (Shove Knife / Mica Slip)

- **Principle:** Depressing the spring latch directly through the door reveal gap.
- **Target Hardware:** Outswing exterior doors or interior office doors where the latch bevel faces the operator.
- **Limitation:** Ineffective if the auxiliary deadlocking pin is properly seated and depressed against the strike plate face.

---

## 3. Request to Exit (REX) Sensor Triggering

- **Principle:** Injecting a thermal or physical perturbation through door gaps into the field of view of interior Request to Exit (PIR) sensors.
- **Target Hardware:** [Magnetic Lock Access Systems](/docs/obstacles/electronic-access).
- **Execution:** Spraying inverted canned air (refrigerant mist) through meeting stiles or door seams to rapidly alter ambient infrared contrast.
