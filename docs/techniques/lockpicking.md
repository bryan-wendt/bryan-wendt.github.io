# Single Pin Picking (SPP) and Raking

Lock picking is the non-destructive manipulation of a cylinder's internal components to reach the shear line without using the original key.

---

## Single Pin Picking (SPP) Methodology

Single Pin Picking relies on microscopic manufacturing tolerances where pin chambers are never drilled in a perfectly straight line. When rotational tension is applied to the plug, **one pin stack binds first**.

### Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Apply Rotational Tension:** Insert a tension tool (Top of Keyway / TOK or Bottom of Keyway / BOK) and apply very light rotational pressure.
2. **Find the Binding Pin:** Probe each pin chamber with a hook pick. Free pins will spring back when pressed; the binding pin will feel rigid and resistant.
3. **Lift to Shear:** Gently lift the binding pin until a distinct click is felt and the plug rotates slightly forward.
4. **Repeat the Binding Order:** Find the next binding pin in the sequence and repeat until all pin stacks are set across the shear line.
5. **Rotate the Plug:** Once the final pin sets, the plug turns freely and unlocks the mechanism.

---

## Raking & Kinetic Techniques

Raking uses wave, worm, or bogota pick profiles scrubbed rapidly across the pin stacks under light, fluctuating tension to bounce driver pins across the shear line simultaneously.

- **Best Suited For:** Standard pin tumbler locks with low security tolerances and mild bitting differentials.
- **Limitations:** Ineffective against high-security spool, serrated, or sidebar mechanisms.
