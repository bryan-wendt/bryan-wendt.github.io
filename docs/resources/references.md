# Security References & Educational Links

Curated collection of foundational literature, industry standards bodies, community lockpicking organizations, and conference presentations in physical penetration testing.

---

## Organizations & Standards Bodies

- [TOOOL (The Open Organisation Of Lockpickers - USA)](https://toool.us/)  
  Non-profit educational association advancing public education in lock picking, mechanical security, and responsible disclosure.
- [TOOOL International (Netherlands)](https://toool.nl/)  
  The original European branch of TOOOL, providing research papers on lock mechanics and forensic examination.
- [ALOA Security Professionals Association (Associated Locksmiths of America)](https://www.aloa.org/)  
  Primary trade association and educational organization for professional locksmiths, safe technicians, and physical security specialists.
- [BHMA (Builders Hardware Manufacturers Association)](https://buildershardware.com/)  
  Standards developer for ANSI/BHMA building hardware standards (ANSI/BHMA A156 series) governing locks, deadbolts, and egress panic devices.
- [ANSI (American National Standards Institute)](https://www.ansi.org/)  
  Coordinates national commercial hardware standards including door assemblies, fire ratings, and security grades (Grade 1, 2, 3).

## Foundational Literature & Guides

- [MIT Guide to Lock Picking (Theodore T. Tool)](https://www.mit.edu/people/jhawk/lockpicking.html)  
  The seminal 1991 paper breaking down the fundamental physics and tolerances of single pin picking (SPP), pin binding order, and shear mechanics.
- [Practical Lock Picking (2nd Edition) - Deviant Ollam](https://deviating.net/words/books/)  
  The definitive modern field training manual on mechanical lock picking, pin tumbler manipulation, security pin navigation, and bypass tooling.
- [Keys to the Kingdom: Impressioning, Privilege Escalation, and Key Duplication - Deviant Ollam](https://deviating.net/words/books/)  
  Comprehensive study on key duplication, over-the-shoulder decoding, optical decoding, casting, and blank impressioning.
- [Locksmithing: Mechanical & Electronic (Bill Phillips)](https://www.mhprofessional.com/)  
  Authoritative textbook covering commercial cylinder servicing, keyway coding, master keying charts, and electric strike installation.
- [High-Security Mechanical Locks: An Encyclopedic Reference (Marc Weber Tobias)](https://security.org/)  
  Exhaustive engineering and forensic analysis of sidebar locks, rotating pins, high-security disc detainer systems, and bypass vulnerabilities.

  High-level breakdown of architectural bypasses, under-door tools, latch slipping, Request to Exit (REX) PIR sensor evasion, and elevator bypasses.
- [Deviant Ollam: "Doors, Locks, and Bypasses" (DEF CON 19)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmsf9xWqS5o)  
  Deep dive into crash bar manipulation, meeting stile gaps, door reveals, and physical security flaws in commercial buildings.
- [Babak Javadi: "The Black Art of Access Control Bypass" (DEF CON)](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Babak+Javadi+DEFCON+physical+security)  
  Explores vulnerabilities in electronic strikes, magnetic locks, credential cloning, and sensor defeating techniques.
- [Marc Weber Tobias: "Bypassing High-Security Locks and Forensics" (DEF CON)](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Marc+Weber+Tobias+DEFCON)  
  Forensic analysis demonstrating bypasses against commercial and institutional high-security cylinders.
- [LockPickingLawyer (YouTube Channel)](https://www.youtube.com/@lockpickinglawyer)  
  Concise, technical demonstrations of non-destructive picking, decoding, shimming, and bypasses across hundreds of lock models.
