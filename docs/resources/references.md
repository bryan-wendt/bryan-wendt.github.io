# Security References & Educational Links

Curated collection of foundational literature, industry standards bodies, community lockpicking organizations, and conference presentations in physical penetration testing.

---

## Organizations & Standards Bodies

- [TOOOL (The Open Organisation Of Lockpickers - USA)](https://toool.us/)  
  Non-profit educational association advancing public education in lock picking, mechanical security, and responsible disclosure.
- [TOOOL International (Netherlands)](https://toool.nl/)  
  The original European branch of TOOOL, providing research papers on lock mechanics and forensic examination.
- [ALOA Security Professionals Association (Associated Locksmiths of America)](https://www.aloa.org/resources)  
  Primary trade association and educational organization for professional locksmiths, safe technicians, and physical security specialists.
- [BHMA (Builders Hardware Manufacturers Association Standards)](https://buildershardware.com/Hardware-Standards/)  
  Standards developer for ANSI/BHMA building hardware standards (ANSI/BHMA A156 series) governing locks, deadbolts, and egress panic devices.
- [ANSI Standards Store](https://webstore.ansi.org/)  
  Coordinates national commercial hardware standards including door assemblies, fire ratings, and security grades (Grade 1, 2, 3).

## Foundational Literature & Guides

- [MIT Guide to Lock Picking (Theodore T. Tool)](https://archive.org/details/mit-guide-to-lock-picking-v05/mode/2up)  
  The seminal 1991 paper breaking down the fundamental physics and tolerances of single pin picking (SPP), pin binding order, and shear mechanics.
- [Practical Lock Picking (2nd Edition) - Deviant Ollam](https://books.google.mn/books?id=YVUif5IYINAC&printsec=copyright#v=onepage&q&f=false)  
  The definitive modern field training manual on mechanical lock picking, pin tumbler manipulation, security pin navigation, and bypass tooling.
- [Keys to the Kingdom: Impressioning, Privilege Escalation, and Key Duplication - Deviant Ollam](https://ia601601.us.archive.org/26/items/keys-to-the-kingdom-impressioning-privilege-escalation-bumping/Deviant%20Ollam%20-%20Keys%20to%20the%20Kingdom_%20Impressioning%2C%20Privilege%20Escalation%2C%20Bumping.pdf)  
  Comprehensive study on key duplication, over-the-shoulder decoding, optical decoding, casting, and blank impressioning.
- [The Complete Book of Locks and Locksmithing (Bill Phillips)](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/157349.The_Complete_Book_of_Locks_and_Locksmithing)  
  Authoritative reference covering commercial cylinder servicing, keyway coding, master keying charts, and electric strike installation.
- [High-Security Mechanical Locks: An Encyclopedic Reference (Graham Pulford)](https://archive.org/details/high-security-mechanical-locks-an-encyclopedic-reference-2007)  
  Exhaustive engineering and forensic analysis of sidebar locks, rotating pins, high-security disc detainer systems, and bypass vulnerabilities.

- [Kaba Ilco Key Blank Directory & Support Resources](https://www.ilco.us/support/resources)  
  The definitive industrial directory for key blank identification, section profiles, cylinder warding cross-references, vehicular keys, and heavy equipment blanks.
- [Allegion Document Library (Schlage Service & Key Systems)](https://us.allegion.com/en/home/service-support/document-library.html)

- [Corbin Russwin Cylinder Manual (Public Archive via Internet Archive)](https://archive.org/details/corbin-russwin-cylinder-manual)

- [dormakaba Technical Documentation & BEST A2 Service Manual](https://dhwsupport.dormakaba.com/hc/en-us/articles/201132377-Mechanical-Product-Service-Manuals)  
  Official service manuals (Doc T61803 / T35527) and basic worksheets (Doc T92841) covering the BEST A-2, A-3, and A-4 Small Format Interchangeable Core systems.
- [Sargent Lock Cylinders & Key Systems Catalog](https://www.sargentlock.com/en/commercial-locks-products/key-systems/)  
  ASSA ABLOY resource for conventional and LFIC cylinder pinning, bitting tolerances, and master keying rules across Sargent keyway families.
- [Master Lock Technical Service Manuals](https://www.masterlock.com/business/technical-service-manuals)  
  Factory manuals (including Document 7000-0031) detailing commercial cylinder specifications, pinning kits (#291), and rekeying procedures.
- [Kwikset Support & Service Documentation](https://www.kwikset.com/support/answers/how-to-re-key-your-smartkey-security-locks)  
  Official pin-tumbler cylinder rekeying guides, plug follower specifications, and SmartKey mechanism overviews.

  Universal pinning charts, .115" standard and .108" SFIC pin dimension specifications, and kit calibration standards.

## Academic & Cryptological Physical Security Papers

- [Matt Blaze: "Cryptology and Physical Security: Rights Amplification in Master-Keyed Mechanical Locks" (2002)](https://www.mattblaze.org/papers/mk.pdf)  
  Seminal research demonstrating mathematical vulnerabilities in hierarchical master key systems, detailing the progression search algorithm to reconstruct Grand Master Keys from single change keys.
- [Matt Blaze: "Safecracking for the Computer Scientist" (2004)](https://www.mattblaze.org/papers/safelocks.pdf)  
  In-depth analysis of mechanical combination safe lock tolerances, contact point graphing, wheel pack physics, and manipulation algorithms.
- [Flavio D. Garcia, Peter van Rossum, Roel Verdult, Ronny Wichers Schreur: "Dismantling iCLASS and iCLASS Elite" (ESORICS 2012)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/235916470_Dismantling_iClass_and_iClass_Elite)  
  Peer-reviewed academic research exposing cryptographic weaknesses in HID iCLASS key diversification and memory dump extraction.
- [ASME A17.1 / CSA B44 Safety Code for Elevators and Escalators (Section 2.27 Emergency Operations)](https://www.asme.org/getmedia/05d3cb98-f573-42f4-b8ad-86a26682091a/a17-1_csa-b44_2019-table-of-contents.pdf)  
  The standardized North American life safety elevator code defining Phase 1 Emergency Recall, Phase 2 In-Car Firefighter Operation, and the FEO-K1 standard key switch.
