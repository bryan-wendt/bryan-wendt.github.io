# Security References & Educational Links

Recommended physical security books, research papers, conference presentations, and community organizations.

---

## Organizations & Standards

- **TOOOL (The Open Organisation Of Lockpickers):** Educational guides, lock picking ethics, and community training.
- **BHMA / ANSI Standards:** Hardware standards governing commercial lock durability, grade ratings, and egress requirements.
- **ALOA (Associated Locksmiths of America):** Professional locksmith standards and trade education.

---

## Classic Reference Material

- *Practical Lock Picking* by Deviant Ollam
- *Keys to the Kingdom* by Deviant Ollam
- *Open Locks Without Keys* by Robert L.
- *Visual Guide to Lock Picking* by Mark McCloud
