# Field Identification Guide

Accurate lock and hardware identification is the first step in determining applicable bypass techniques, tooling requirements, and defensive strength during an engagement.

---

## 4-Step Identification Workflow

When approaching a lock in the field, evaluate the hardware in the following sequence:

```text
1. Cylinder Format & Housing (Mortise, Rim, Deadbolt, Padlock, Euro Profile)
   ↓
2. Keyway Shape & Orientation (Standard Paracentric, Open, Restricted, Dimple)
   ↓
3. Manufacturer Markings & Branding (Schlage, Kwikset, Yale, Corbin Russwin, Best)
   ↓
4. Internal Mechanism Type (Pin Tumbler, Wafer, Tubular, Disc Detainer, SFIC)
```

## Common Cylinder Types

![Common Cylinder Formats and Identifying Characteristics](/img/wiki/identification/cylinder-formats.svg)
*Figure 1: Comparison of primary cylinder formats, mounting styles, and identifying exterior traits. Source & Reference: [ALOA International Locksmith Standards & BHMA Guidelines](/docs/resources/references).*

| Cylinder Type | Common Locations | Exterior Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mortise Cylinder** | Commercial storefronts, glass aluminum doors | Threaded exterior body with a cam on the rear |
| **Rim Cylinder** | Panic exit bars, surface-mounted deadlatches | Smooth body secured from the rear with two mounting bolts |
| **Key-in-Knob (KIK)** | Commercial and residential lever handles | Integrated directly inside the handle or knob rosette |
| **Euro Profile** | European doors, multi-point lock systems | Figure-eight profile with a central rotating cam |
| **Small Format IC (SFIC)** | Universities, corporate offices, hospitals | Figure-eight faceplate with interchangeable core capability |

## Keyway Taxonomy

![Keyway Warding Profiles Comparison](/img/wiki/identification/keyway-profiles.svg)
*Figure 2: Open vs Paracentric vs Restricted keyway warding profiles and pick clearance. Source & Reference: [TOOOL Keyway Specifications & Deviant Ollam Research](/docs/resources/references).*

Keyway profiles dictate the type and thickness of picks, decoders, or bump keys that can be inserted into the plug:

- **Open / Standard Keyways:** Broad openings with minimal warding (e.g., Kwikset KW1, Master Lock #1). Easily accessible with standard 0.025" pick profiles.
- **Paracentric Keyways:** Curved warding that extends past the center line of the keyway (e.g., Schlage SC1/SC4, Yale Y1). Often requires thinner 0.020" or 0.015" picks.
- **Restricted / Patented Keyways:** High-security profiles with active sidebar channels or custom milling (e.g., Medeco, Schlage Primus, Mul-T-Lock).
